Kiln

A shader written from a kiln, not from a pack.

Light is a fire that cools as it travels. Noon is white heat. Dusk is the mouth of the kiln, orange on the horizon and blue ash overhead. Night keeps only the coals: moon, torch, lantern, lava. Water is a dark glass that remembers the sky. Rain thickens the air until shafts of light become visible. Distant land fades into the same heat haze as the sky, so the horizon is one material.

Iris 1.8 or newer. Minecraft 1.21.11.

Settings live under Look, Light, World, and Image. Ember is the intended grade. Ash cools it. Clear spends less.
